List tracked Chimera propagation obligations.

Use this tool when you need to inspect pending, claimed, stale, resolved, or ignored obligations for the current project, typically before final closeout.

Do NOT use it to create or update obligations — use `chimera_obligations_sync` and the single-obligation tools. `refresh` (default true) may mark obligations stale when referenced graph nodes disappeared.

After this tool:
- Claim, resolve, or ignore active obligations relevant to the task before final closeout.
