Claim one tracked Chimera obligation.

Use this tool when you are taking responsibility for reviewing or updating a specific obligation, normally before editing its target.

Do NOT use it to create obligations or choose from candidates — use `chimera_obligations_sync` first when findings need tracking. Prefer `ref` copied from `Ref: obligation:<id>` output; `obligationID` is the legacy path.

After this tool:
- Review or update the target, then resolve or ignore the obligation with a reason.
